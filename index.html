Progression: Move to the next phase when every set feels clean and the last rep matches the first. Minimum 3–4 weeks per phase.
Session order: Always start with Dead bug and Glute bridge. Finish with planks. Rest 45–60 sec between sets in Phase 1, 30–45 sec in Phases 2–3.
Phase 1 — Foundation
Weeks 1–4 · ~15 min per session
Goal: Activate the deep core — learn to brace, zero pain
Dead bug ⭐
Most important move — master this before anything else
2–3 sets5 reps/side
Low back glued to floor. Extend opposite arm and leg slowly — 3 sec down.
Glute bridge
Weak glutes are a primary driver of lower back pain
2–3 sets10 reps
Feet flat, drive hips up, squeeze glutes and hold 2 sec at top.
Modified plank (knees down)
Progress to full plank in Phase 2
3 sets20 sec
Hollow your belly button in. Don't just hold — actively squeeze everything.
Bird dog
Move slow. Speed is the enemy here
2–3 sets5 reps/side
On all fours, extend opposite arm and leg. Hips stay perfectly level.
Side-lying hip abduction
Hits lateral stabilizers most people ignore
2–3 sets10 reps/side
Straight leg raises lying on your side. Hips stacked, don't roll back.

Weeks 5–8 · ~20 min per session
Goal: Time under tension — add anti-rotation, longer holds

Same move as Phase 1 — just slower and more reps

Significantly harder than standard bridge — go slow
3 sets8 reps/side
One foot flat, other leg extended. Drive through heel, keep hips level.
Bird dog with 3-sec hold
3 sets8 reps/side
Hold the extended position 3 full seconds before returning. No wobble.

Hardest move of this phase — start with 10 sec if needed
3 sets15–20 sec
Flat on back, arms overhead, press low back into floor, lift shoulders and legs slightly.

Weeks 9–12 · ~25 min per session
Goal: Loaded and dynamic — real core strength under resistance

Same movement — now with load. Start very light.

Hold KB straight up over chest while alternating leg lowers. Low back flat.
Full plank

Squeeze everything. If form breaks before time is up, stop and rest.
Side plank with hip dip
3 sets10 dips/side
From full side plank, lower hip to floor and drive back up. Controlled movement.

3 sets12 reps/side
Add a 2-sec hold at top for extra glute activation.
KB march — standing
Direct carry-over to your KB circuit workouts
3 sets40 sec

3 sets15 reps/side
Feet off floor, rotate controlled — don't swing. Hold KB or DB for load.
Windmill (light KB)
Most complex move in the program — prioritize form over weight always
3 sets5 reps/side
KB overhead, eyes on the bell, push hip out to side as you hinge down. Go very light.
Back pain note: If anything causes actual pain (not burn) in your lower back, stop that movement. Dead bug and bird dog are spine-safe — lean on those heavily in Phase 1. Pain should reduce noticeably around weeks 3–4 as your TVA strengthens. If it persists, see a professional.

Connect Google Sheets

Step 1 — Create a Google Sheet

Your exercise library lives in a Google Sheet. The app reads it live — add an exercise to the sheet and it appears here instantly.

Make a copy of the template sheet, or create your own with these columns:

  1. A — Exercise (name)
  2. B — Category (Full body / Lower / Upper / Core / Cardio)
  3. C — Equipment (display name, e.g. "KB + box")
  4. D — Equip. Tag (must be exactly: KB / Bench / DB / Barbell / Bodyweight / Cardio)
  5. E — Key Note
  6. F — Sets
  7. G — Reps / Time

Row 1 = headers. Data starts row 2. Sheet name must be KB Library or Full Library.

Step 2 — Get an API Key

  1. Go to console.cloud.google.com
  2. Create a new project (or select existing)
  3. Go to APIs & Services → Library
  4. Search for Google Sheets API and enable it
  5. Go to APIs & Services → Credentials
  6. Click Create Credentials → API Key
  7. Copy the key and paste it below

Step 3 — Get your Sheet ID

Open your Google Sheet. The URL looks like:

docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/SHEET_ID_HERE/edit

Copy the long ID between /d/ and /edit and paste it below.

Then share your sheet: click Share → Anyone with the link → Viewer.
